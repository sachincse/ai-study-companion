# Sample notes: real, openly licensed textbooks

The sample corpus is made of complete open textbooks, not synthetic text. Run
`python scripts/fetch_open_notes.py` to download them into this folder (they are
git-ignored because of their size). Each one maps to an AKTU subject so the demo,
the benchmark and the tutor are exercised on genuine course material.

| Book | Author(s) | Licence | AKTU subject | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Open Data Structures (Edition 0.1G, pseudocode) | Pat Morin | CC BY 2.5 | BCS301 Data Structure, BCS503 DAA | https://opendatastructures.org |
| Database Design - 2nd Edition | Adrienne Watt, Nelson Eng (BCcampus) | CC BY 4.0 | BCS501 DBMS | https://opentextbc.ca/dbdesign01/ |
| Operating Systems and Middleware: Supporting Controlled Interaction (rev 1.2, archive.org mirror of the author's free edition) | Max Hailperin | CC BY-SA 3.0 (see licence page in the PDF) | BCS401 Operating System | https://gustavus.edu/+max/os-book/ |
| Discrete Mathematics: An Open Introduction (3rd ed.) | Oscar Levin | CC BY-SA 4.0 | BCS303 Discrete Structures | https://discrete.openmathbooks.org |

Attribution notice: these works are used unmodified, as uploaded study material,
under their respective Creative Commons licences. Copyright remains with the
authors. If you redistribute derived material (summaries, flashcards) generated
from the CC BY-SA books, the share-alike terms apply to that material.

Students can of course upload their own notes instead; nothing in the app
depends on these files.
